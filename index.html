Research

Eligibility Design and Participation Exit in Carbon Offset Futures, with Ying Liu
Working paper · FMA Europe 2026 · SSRN 6460979

Exchange-traded futures on voluntary carbon credits whose deliverable grade buyers cannot verify. A regime-switching framework traces how information moved across carbon markets after the shocks that broke public trust, and a cheapest-to-deliver pricing framework explains why the price collapse was common to all three contracts. It grew out of my MSc research project, which won the Best Research Project Prize.
