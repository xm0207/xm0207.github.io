Much of my research is done with AI. These notes collect what I have learned along the way, and a few news items from the fields I work in.

The Idea

Consistency

The same habits on every project, so that the work can be checked and repeated.

Easy to Start

Set-ups that someone new to these tools can pick up quickly.

Built to Last

Tools are replaced quickly as mainstream AI moves on. What lasts is how a person and an AI divide the work, and that lives in your head, not in the tool.

In the News

Recent items related to my research. Each links to the original source.

  • Verra · 28 September 2026 · Carbon Markets
    Verra Secures ICVCM Approval for Carbon Capture and Storage Methodology

    The Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market approved Verra's methodology VM0049 and its modules for direct air capture, CO₂ transport, CO₂ storage and bioenergy with carbon capture as meeting the Core Carbon Principles.

  • ICVCM · Open 22 October to 4 December 2026 · Carbon Markets
    Consultation: Clarifications Related to Government Administered Programs

    The Integrity Council is consulting on clarifications to its Core Carbon Principles rules.

  • InsuranceIndustry.ai · 23 October 2026 · AI in Insurance
    NAIC Exposes Version 6.0 of Its AI Risk Evaluation Supplement

    The US insurance supervisors' Big Data and AI Working Group put version 6.0 out for comment on 16 October, with comments due 30 October. The draft adds a question on whether a high-risk use case involves agentic AI, and lets regulators leave some generalized linear models out of certain requests.

  • Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research · July 2026 · AI and Labour
    What Is Really Happening to Jobs? Separating AI Hype from Reality

    A policy brief concluding that AI's effect on aggregate employment is probably small for now, that it may be part of why recent graduates find hiring tough, and that its effect on worker productivity is mixed but generally positive.
