Checking Corporate Emissions Reporting

Summer research at the Centre for Climate Finance & Investment, Imperial College London. I supervised the interns who carried it out.

All Projects

A reported emissions figure is only as good as the weakest link in the chain behind it. Two companies can report the same physical emission and publish different numbers, legitimately, because of the warming-potential vintage, the Scope 3 method and the unit. The project checked each link in turn.

What Was Checked

CheckQuestionHow
UnitIs the figure in the unit it claims?Compare its magnitude with the report's own totals and with similar firms
GWP BasisWhich IPCC assessment supplied the warming potentials?Divide reported tCO₂e by the mass of each gas to recover the factor actually applied, and match it to published IPCC values
Scope 3 MethodHow were indirect emissions estimated?Read the method the company states for each Scope 3 category
MagnitudeIs the value plausible at all?Flag outliers and review each against its source report
AssuranceDid anyone check the figure, and how strictly?Read the assurance statement: the level of assurance, the standard, the boundary and the materiality threshold

The results, including the coverage, the data-quality review and the assurance analysis, are shared with the project's stakeholders through a dashboard.
