BEcon Thesis

Analysis of Factors Driving Carbon Market Prices

Shanghai University of Finance and Economics · 2024
China runs regional pilot carbon markets alongside its national market. The thesis asks what moves allowance prices on each.

Read the Thesis (PDF) All Research

The thesis studies how carbon markets developed and how their prices behave, first across the world and then on China's regional and national exchanges, where it asks whether market-wide, industry and energy factors explain allowance returns.

Key Findings

  1. Carbon prices move with the business cycle. Across the world's carbon markets, prices are pro-cyclical, and allowances account for most of the market.
  2. Industry factors matter, and so does the region. In China, the market premium and industry factors (metallurgy, aviation, broad agriculture) explain allowance returns, but regional effects are significant.
  3. Regional and national exchanges differ. On the regional exchanges, prices move more strongly against energy prices; on the national exchange, returns depend more on industry factors than on the market premium.
